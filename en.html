📺 Watch TV

📺 Switch on the TV with sound

Orange Preamp TV
  1. Check that the lounge speakers are switched on.
  2. Switch on the TV — TV remote, ⏻ button.
  3. Switch on the Orange box — Orange remote, ⏻ button.
  4. Switch on the preamp — preamp remote, ⏻ button.
  5. On the preamp, press Source / Input until it shows Optical In.
  6. Set the sound with both remotes: Orange VOL and the preamp volume.
That’s it The TV shows the Orange box automatically. Now choose what to watch below.

🔢 TV channels

Orange
  1. Type the channel number (e.g. 2).
  2. Change channel: P + / P −.

🎬 Netflix

Orange
  1. Press MENU.
  2. With the arrows, go down to the Netflix tile.
  3. Press OK.

🏰 Disney+

Orange
  1. Press MENU.
  2. With the arrows, go to the Disney+ tile (same as Netflix).
  3. Press OK.

▶️ YouTube

Orange
  1. Press MENU.
  2. With the arrows, go up and open More apps.
  3. Find YouTube then press OK.

🎥 Rent a film (VOD)

Orange
  1. Press MENU.
  2. With the arrows, go up to VOD.
  3. Choose the film and follow the screen.
Careful — this costs money Each rental is charged to the Orange account.

🎵 Music & sound

💿 Play a CD

CD playerPreamp
  1. Speakers and preamp switched on.
  2. Switch on the CD player and put the disc in.
  3. Set the preamp to Line In (Source / Input button).
  4. Press ▶ Play. Volume: preamp.

📱 Phone (Bluetooth)

Preamp
  1. Speakers and preamp switched on.
  2. Set the preamp to Bluetooth (Source / Input button).
  3. On the phone: Bluetooth → connect to Salon.
  4. Start the music. Volume: phone + preamp.

🔊 Sound in the entrance / dining room

Entrance preamp
  1. Check that the 2 entrance speakers are switched on.
  2. Switch on the entrance preamp.
  3. The lounge sound (TV, CD, Bluetooth) plays in the entrance too.
To stop Switch off the entrance preamp. The lounge carries on as normal.

🛠️ If something goes wrong

No sound
  • Are the speakers switched on? (lounge — and entrance if needed)
  • Is the preamp switched on and set to the right input? (Optical In for the TV)
  • Is the Orange volume above zero? Is the preamp volume turned up? You need both.
No picture (black screen)
  • Are the TV and the Orange box switched on?
  • The TV must be on the right HDMI input (the Orange box one). On the TV remote: Source / Input, then choose the Orange box HDMI.
The TV remote doesn’t change the volume

This is on purpose: the TV volume is blocked. Use Orange (VOL) and the preamp volume.

Sound in the lounge but not in the entrance
  • Switch on the entrance preamp.
  • Check the 2 entrance speakers.
Bluetooth won’t connect
  • Set the preamp to the Bluetooth input.
  • On the phone: “forget” Salon, then reconnect.
The sound stutters or cuts out
  • Check that nothing has been moved on the devices.
  • Switch the Orange box off and on again.

⚡ Before you start

  • The speakers always stay on (lounge + entrance).
  • For the TV, switch on 3 devices: the TV, the Orange box and the preamp.
  • The volume is set with 2 remotes: Orange + preamp (not the TV remote).

📺 Watch TV

  1. Switch on TV + Orange + preamp.
  2. Preamp on the “Optical In” input (Source button).
  3. Set the volume: Orange + preamp.
  • Channels: type the number · change with P+ / P−.
  • Netflix / Disney+: MENU → arrows → the tile → OK.
  • YouTube: MENU → up → “More apps” → YouTube.
  • Rent a film: MENU → “VOD” (chargeable).

🎵 Listen to music

  • CD: preamp on “Line In”, then Play.
  • Phone (Bluetooth): preamp on “Bluetooth”; on the phone, connect to “Salon”.
  • Sound in the entrance: switch on the entrance preamp.

🛠️ If something goes wrong

  • No sound: speakers on? preamp on the right input? Orange volume not at zero and preamp turned up?
  • No picture: TV + Orange on? TV on the right HDMI input (TV Source/Input button).
  • The TV volume doesn’t work: that’s normal — use Orange + preamp.
  • Nothing in the entrance: switch on the entrance preamp.
  • Bluetooth: preamp on “Bluetooth”; “forget” Salon then reconnect.
