Playing discs

▶ Watch TV

  1. Turn on all 3 remotes 1 2 3 (the on/off button on each).
  2. On 2 Sound: press the source button (⤴) until the round screen shows Optical In.
  3. Volume: adjust with 1 and 2.
⚠ No sound? The 3 Screen remote never changes the volume — it’s 1 and 2. Also check that neither one is turned down to zero.
Change channel: type the number on 1, or use the + / − keys.

🎬 Netflix · Disney+ · YouTube

  • On 1 TV & Apps, press MENU.
  • Use the arrows to reach the tile (Netflix, Disney+…) then OK.
  • YouTube: MENU → « Autres applis » → YouTube.

🎵 Listen to music

  • A CD: turn on 4 (red button), insert the disc. On 2, source (⤴) → Line In, then ▶.
  • Your phone: on 2, source (⤴) → Bluetooth. On the phone, connect to the Bluetooth called Salon.

🛠️ If something isn’t working

  • No sound at all: are the speakers switched on (switch to I on the back)? Is 2 Sound on the right source? Is the volume on 1 and 2 turned up?
  • Bluetooth won’t connect: on the phone, “forget” Salon then reconnect.
  • No picture (black screen): are 3 Screen and 1 Orange on? If not, on 3 Screen, change the source (Source button) until you see the TV.
  • Sound drops out / stutters: switch the 1 Orange box off and on again.
